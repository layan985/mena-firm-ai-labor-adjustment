# Identification question: exposure or adoption?

I originally wanted first documented AI adoption to be the treatment date. I do not think I can defend that timing as exogenous. Firms choose when to adopt, and that choice may respond to expected demand, restructuring, management quality, or labor costs.

I am therefore separating two analyses:

1. A stronger design interacts a pre-2022 exposure measure with time after the release of ChatGPT. The estimand is differential response to an external shock by prior exposure.
2. A staggered event-time analysis describes what happens around documented adoption. I will not call it the causal effect of adoption unless additional assumptions and diagnostics become defensible.

Using Sun–Abraham or Callaway–Sant'Anna addresses contamination from heterogeneous treatment effects. It does not make voluntary treatment timing exogenous. That distinction stays in the README and paper even if the adoption graph looks clean.
