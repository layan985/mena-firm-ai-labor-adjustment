# Employment conflict: stc and Almarai

The first employment extraction made it clear that “headcount” is not one variable.

stc reports 22,751 group employees in 2023 and 19,863 in 2024. The 2023 report also says a three-year workforce right-sizing plan would begin in 2024. I cannot tell from the two numbers alone how much of the decline is labor adjustment and how much is a change in group perimeter, discontinued operations, or reporting definition.

I added a structural-break flag for annual headcount changes of at least 10%. The flag does not delete the row. It stops the row from entering estimation until I write down what changed and whether the two years measure the same population.

Almarai creates a different problem. Its business discussion says the workforce exceeds 50,000, while the employee-benefit disclosure reports 46,997 GCC employees in 2025 and 43,918 in 2024. I am keeping the narrower series because it has an explicit and comparable definition. The headline remains source context, not a substitute observation.
