# First AI-labeling problem

My first rule was to search annual reports for AI terms and assign the earliest substantive hit as the adoption year. The first firms showed why that rule is unsafe.

Aramco already describes bringing AI into its core business in its 2020 report. That proves AI activity by 2020; it does not prove that adoption began in 2020. The treatment date is left-censored until earlier reports are reviewed.

Almarai shows the opposite error. Its 2024 report discusses generative AI and decision intelligence as technologies to be deployed over the next few years. Keyword matching would call that adoption. I code it as planning because there is no identified operational use in the excerpt. The 2025 report describes expanded AI productivity tools and an AI Centre of Excellence, which is provisionally operational evidence but still needs tool-level review.

Decision: the rules engine generates candidates only. A human must read the excerpt, identify the business function, distinguish plans from deployment, and record whether earlier documents were searched.
