# Results

Last updated: 10 August 2026.

## Empirical hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-2022 AI exposure changes post-2022 employment growth | Not tested | The firm-year panel is not complete. |
| Employment changes around first operational AI adoption | Not tested | Adoption dates and comparison groups are not yet validated. |
| Adjustment is concentrated in particular worker groups | Not tested | Comparable composition outcomes have not been collected at scale. |

There are no causal coefficients, event-study figures, or p-values to report.

## What the pilot did establish

The seed exposed several observations that cannot be used naively:

| Firm or record | Problem | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aramco 2020 AI disclosure | Substantive use is already visible, so the beginning of adoption is unobserved | Keep for exposure and descriptive work; exclude from a 2020 adoption cohort |
| stc 2023–2024 headcount | Large decline coincides with a right-sizing plan and possible perimeter changes | Flag until reporting scope and transactions are reconciled |
| Almarai workforce headline | “50,000+” and the GCC employee-benefit population measure different groups | Do not splice the headline into the comparable series |
| Almarai 2024 AI language | Describes technology planned for future deployment | Code as planning, not adoption |

These are measurement decisions, not evidence that AI raised or reduced employment.
