\begin{abstract}
This paper studies how firm-level artificial-intelligence adoption and exposure are associated with labor-market adjustment across Middle Eastern and North African firms. We combine transparent text-based measurement of substantive AI deployment with firm employment outcomes and modern difference-in-differences/event-study methods. The empirical design explicitly distinguishes exposure to the generative-AI shock from endogenous firm adoption and reports dynamic estimates, pre-trend diagnostics, and extensive robustness checks.
\end{abstract}
