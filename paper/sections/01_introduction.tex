\section{Introduction}
Artificial intelligence is diffusing into firms faster than standard firm surveys can measure it. This measurement problem is particularly severe in developing and emerging economies. Recent work demonstrates that World Bank Enterprise Survey innovation narratives can identify AI use in MENAAP firms \citep{udomsaph2026}. That work explicitly identifies employment dynamics as a next research frontier. We extend the question from \emph{who adopts} to \emph{what happens to labor when AI becomes operational}.
